## Verdict (D, E, F)

1. **F is cleanest on the hard code numbers** (1 Blocker, the shared ICF wall; stair headroom 2,001 mm; roof spans 8.72 m; no well under a deck or porch), **but it is the largest structure:** 413 ft of 3.09 m ICF wall, 24 corners, a 165 sq ft roof-table gap, a short stair-foot landing (229 mm), the thinnest garage bracing (22% solid) and a shaft built against the perimeter wall.
2. **D is next:** 8.63 m roof spans and a compact perimeter, but two small plan fixes to make (bedroom 3's well under the deck, 30 mm headroom margin) plus a 16 ft garage header and five unwelled windows.
3. **E is weakest on structure:** a stair headroom code failure as drawn (1,894 mm) and a 39 ft clear span with no marked bearing line (11.9 m, 2 m over the lintel-table basis). On code alone, F then D then E; the shared items (ICF wall at 3.09 m, garage door heights, elevator pit and overrun) need the engineer in all three.
