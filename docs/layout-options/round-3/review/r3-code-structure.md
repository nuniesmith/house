## Verdict

1. **On the hard code numbers H is level with F and ahead of D:** one shared Blocker (the 3.09 m ICF wall), stair headroom 2,001 mm, full roof coverage, no well under a deck or porch, drive grade about 2.7% instead of 9.4%, the elevator 6.6 ft from the ICF wall, and the shortest basement wall of the three (283.8 ft against D 318 and F 413). It is a bigger build than D above grade (3,571 sq ft, 22 corners).
2. **Its worst items are plan errors, cheap to fix:** the stair foot is walled off (CS-02, a door row), the mudroom door is in a corner (CS-07), the bedroom 3 well is 0.5 ft short (CS-11), the louvres have no wells (CS-10), the clerestory has 0.43 ft of height (CS-04).
3. **The structure cost H adds is the unexcavated side:** 1,019 sq ft of floor with its underside 4.5 in above grade, 124.5 ft of frost wall, 118.8 ft of retaining ICF wall, plus the 11.4 m quiet-wing truss span with a bearing line that does not sit over the basement wall (CS-03, CS-09). The engineer should see those before the layout is chosen.
