Method notes. Room dimensions are inside dimensions from the PF files (inches as printed, metric in parentheses = in x 0.0254). Where a "shorter dimension" is not printed, I derived it (area / printed wall) or measured it on the 150-dpi Rooms sheet (+/- 12 in); each is labelled. Roof framing is not drawn in any set, so "roof span" means the clear room width a roof member would have to cross if it ran across that width; the roof direction is not shown. Part 9 limits used (all quoted in the Citation log): floor joists, Table 9.23.4.2.-A max 5.66 m; roof rafters, Tables F/G max 10.96 m at 1.0 kPa down to 7.60 m at 3.0 kPa; Part 9 clear-span cap 12.2 m (9.4.2.1.(1)); lintel tables valid only where trusses span 9.8 m or less (9.23.12.3.(1)).
