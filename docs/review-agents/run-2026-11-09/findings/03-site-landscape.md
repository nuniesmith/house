# Site / Landscape review (prefix SITE)

**Specialist:** Site / Landscape (Wave 2). **Date:** 2026-11-09. **Repo:** `main` @ `d20a2fa`, read-only.
**Inputs read:** COMMON-RULES.md, 03-site-landscape.md brief, plan_facts.md (PF §2, §8.5, §12, §13, §15), `models/site.scad` (phase "house" and "full", rendered to `$RUN/work/SITE/site_top.png` and `site_full.png`), `models/shop.scad` (heights, bays), `docs/shop/Shop-Base-Plan.md` (Nov 3 note, A1-A8, wash bay), `docs/permit/README.md` §1.2, §2, §2.1, §4.5, `docs/house/Redesign-and-Spec-2026-11-02.md` (levels, drainage), `docs/house/House-Review-and-Research-2026-10-28.md` (geothermal §2.2, snow melt §3), Kayla's items K-005, K-011..K-015, K-023, K-335, and the code library (Sewage SOP 2021, ABC 2023 §9.14).

**Standing assumptions for every finding:** land is not chosen, so slope, soils, water table, prevailing wind, road side and which utilities exist are assumptions. Septic and geothermal items are for the PSDS designer and a licensed Alberta engineer. Distances below were recomputed from `site.scad` rectangles with shapely and agree with PF §12 (see the cross-check table). Where a rule is not in the library it says "not found in library".

**Headline:** the house-phase site plan is a plan-position diagram on a flat ground slab (`grade_z = -2` everywhere, site.scad:81, 195). It has no grading, no snow storage, no drainage destinations, no loop field and no parents' house. The septic and well separations pass with large margins. The weaknesses are what is undecided: where water goes, where snow goes, and what is protected after the drawing is forgotten.
