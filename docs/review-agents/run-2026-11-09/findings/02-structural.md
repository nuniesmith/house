**Method notes.**
- Metric conversions are mine (ft x 0.3048).
- Floor spans are centre-line to centre-line between the supports in the model (ICF, bearing lines, frost walls). The span axis per zone is the drawn S1.1 concept (`make_drawings.py:850-864`), not a model fact.
- The model draws every main-floor wall at 0.4 ft and does not mark bearing walls. Which main-floor walls carry roof load is therefore unknown (STR-13).
- Land is not chosen. Snow, soil, groundwater and frost depth are assumptions.

## Part 9 vs Part 4

- Part 9 applies to buildings of 3 storeys or less and no more than 600 m² of building area (1.3.3.3.(1)). Above 600 m², Group C residential buildings fall under Parts 3, 4, 5 and 6 (1.3.3.2.(1)(c)).
- If the house goes to Part 3/4: the whole structure (foundation, floor framing, roof, bracing, connections) is designed to Part 4 loads by an engineer. It no longer gets the Part 9 prescriptive span tables as an option. The README says this too (§1.1).
- If it stays in Part 9: STR-01 to STR-03, STR-06, STR-07 and the stair-opening header are still beyond the tables, so engineered design is needed for those elements either way (9.4.1.1). The 600 m² decision changes the scope of the engineer's work and the fire and egress requirements (for the other specialists), not whether the basement and the pool-room floor need an engineer.
- Part 9 also fixes the way snow is calculated (9.4.2.2) and a minimum roof snow load of 1 kPa.
